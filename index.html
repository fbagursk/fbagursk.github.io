⊢ research

May 2026 – presentBoston, MA

Sort Inference in Pure Type Systems

BU UROP Student Research Award · advised by Professor Nathan Mull

Pure type systems are a family of typed lambda calculi that generalizes the λ-cube. A standing question is which of them are non-normalizing, meaning some well-typed program never finishes evaluating. The best-known example is Girard's paradox in the system λU.

I developed a constraint-based variant of the typing rules, solved by unification, that derives the weakest system able to carry out a given derivation, along with a prototype that does the derivation automatically. Applied to Girard's paradox, it produced a non-normalizing system strictly weaker than the canonical λU−.

Manuscript in preparation. I am presenting this work at the BU UROP Symposium in October 2026.

Course project · CS 392: Rust, in Theory and in Practice

A survey of the connections between modal logic, linear logic, and Rust's ownership and borrowing system.
